### I am a PhD student of Computer Science at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I was coadvised by Professor [Gokul Ravi](https://gsravi.engin.umich.edu/) and Professor [Ang Chen](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chenang/). I study quantum computing system and architecture. My current interests include Variational Quantum Algorithm, NISQ error mitigation and supression, Measurement based QC and classical support for QPU.
