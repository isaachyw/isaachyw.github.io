#### I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I am co-advised by Professor [Gokul Ravi](https://gsravi.engin.umich.edu/) and Professor [Ang Chen](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chenang/). I study quantum computing systems and architecture. My current interests include Variational Quantum Algorithms, NISQ error mitigation and suppression, measurement-based quantum computing, and classical support for QPUs.

I obtained my bachelor's degree from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) and the [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/), where I had the fortune to work with Prof. [Baris Kasikci](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~baris/) on efficient data center processor design, Prof. [Brendan Kochunas](https://ners.engin.umich.edu/people/kochunas-brendan/) on HPC for neutron transport calculations, and Prof. [Tim Byrnes](https://nyu.timbyrnes.net/) on measurement-based photonic quantum computing.
