### I am a PhD student of Computer Science at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor. I am coadvised by Professor [Gokul Ravi](https://gsravi.engin.umich.edu/) and Professor [Ang Chen](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~chenang/). I study quantum computing system and architecture. My current interests include Variational Quantum Algorithm, NISQ error mitigation and supression, Measurement based QC and classical support for QPU.

I obtained my bachelor degree from [Shanghai Jiaotong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) and [University of Michigan](https://umich.edu/), where I have fortune work with Prof. [Baris Kasikci](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~baris/) on efficient data center processor design and Prof. [Brendan Kochunas](https://ners.engin.umich.edu/people/kochunas-brendan/) on HPC for neutron transport calculations, Prof [Tim Byrnes](https://nyu.timbyrnes.net/) on measurement based photonic quantum computing.
